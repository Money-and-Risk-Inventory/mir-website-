Frequently asked questions

What is Money & Risk Inventory (MRI)?

MRI is a financial psychology platform for advisors. It measures how comfortable each client is with risk, how much risk their finances can support, and the psychology behind their decisions.

What is MRI Couples?

MRI Couples is an add-on to MRI Core that shows both partners' risk and money psychology side by side, so advisors can see where they differ and start the conversation.

Does MRI work outside the US?

Yes. Advisors in Canada, the UK, Australia and South Africa use or ask about MRI, especially Risk Capacity. See MRI Around the World.

Can MRI be translated into other languages?

Yes. Translations are offered in most languages and can be customized for your firm.
